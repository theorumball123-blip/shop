Plain English, because nobody reads the other kind. This is what you're agreeing to when you place an order. If something here doesn't seem fair, tell me and we'll sort it out rather than argue about a webpage.

Last updated September 2026

01 Who you're buying from

First Layer Prints is a small home business run from Sumner, Christchurch, New Zealand. Everything is made by one person on one 3D printer.

The business is operated with parental oversight, and a parent or guardian is responsible for it. If you need to raise something formally, ask and you'll be put in touch with them.

02 Prices and payment

All prices are in New Zealand dollars and include any delivery charge shown at the time you order. Prices can change, but never after you've placed an order — what you were quoted is what you pay.

No money is taken through this website. There's no card form and no payment page. You pay when the thing reaches you, in cash or by bank transfer.

If you don't pay for an order you asked for, that's the end of it — no debt collecting, but I probably won't take another order from you.

03 Placing an order

Pressing Place order sends me a request. It isn't a binding sale until I reply and confirm it. That's deliberate: sometimes I'm out of a colour, or the printer is tied up for days on something big.

If I can't do your order, I'll tell you and nothing is owed by anyone.

You can cancel any time before I start printing. Once it's printing, a made-to-order item can't really be un-made, so cancellation after that is up to me — just ask.

04 How long it takes

The times on the site are estimates, not promises. Most things are two to four days. Big items like the 10×10 slime take about a week because they're a full day of printing on their own.

Prints fail sometimes — that's normal in 3D printing, not carelessness. If yours fails I'll start it again and tell you about the delay.

05 What you'll actually get

These are 3D printed, one at a time, by a machine building up plastic in layers. That means:

  • You'll see fine layer lines. That's what printed plastic looks like, not a fault.
  • Rainbow and tri-colour filaments change colour gradually along the print, so no two are identical and yours won't match the photo exactly.
  • Small marks where the print met the bed, or a slightly rougher side, are normal. Where one side is noticeably rougher, the product page says so and shows a photo.
  • Sizes are as listed but can vary by a fraction of a millimetre.

If what arrives is genuinely different from what was described, that's on me — see clause 8.

06 Delivery

Collection at school is free. Otherwise the delivery price is worked out from your address and shown before you order, from $3 in Sumner up to $100 for the rest of the world.

Give me an address I can actually find. If a delivery fails because the address was wrong, a second attempt costs the delivery fee again.

For anything posted, I'll do my best to pack it safely, but once it's with the courier or NZ Post I can't control what happens to it. If it arrives broken, tell me and see clause 8.

07 Looking after it, and safety

These are printed in PLA plastic. They're desk and shelf objects, not tools and not safety equipment.

  • Not suitable for children under three. Small parts can break off and be swallowed.
  • Keep them out of hot cars and off sunny windowsills — PLA softens around 60 °C and will sag.
  • Not dishwasher safe, not food safe, not for use in a mouth.
  • Thin parts — dragon tails, handlebars — will snap if you bend them hard enough.

08 If something's wrong

Tell me. That's the whole process. Message me with what happened and ideally a photo.

If it arrives broken, arrives faulty, or breaks during normal use, I'll reprint it free. If I can't or you'd rather not, you get your money back.

If you broke it doing something it was obviously never meant to do, I'll still usually do you a replacement at cost. Just be honest about what happened.

Because everything is made to order for you specifically, there's no change-of-mind returns — if you decide you don't want a rainbow dragon after I've printed one, I'm stuck with a rainbow dragon. Cancel before printing starts and that's not a problem.

None of this reduces your rights under the New Zealand Consumer Guarantees Act 1993. Those rights apply whatever this page says.

09 Custom prints and other people's designs

You can send me a link to a model you've found and ask whether I can print it. I'll tell you if it'll work on my printer and what it'd cost.

But I'll only print designs where the licence allows it. A lot of free models are marked non-commercial, which means I'm not allowed to print them for money — and models of real branded products often can't be sold at all. If yours is one of those, I'll say no and explain why. Asking nicely doesn't change the licence.

If you send me your own design, it stays yours. I'll print it and won't sell it to anyone else.

10 Your information

When you order, I get your name, your email, your order, and your address if you asked for delivery. That's used to make and deliver your order and nothing else.

I don't sell it, share it, or send marketing. There's no tracking or advertising on this site. The shop remembers your name and past orders in your own browser so you don't retype them — that stays on your device and I never see it. Clear your browser data and it's gone.

Want your details deleted from my email? Ask and I'll delete them.
